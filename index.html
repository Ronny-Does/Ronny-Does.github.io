HyperFrames

A private video tool.

HyperFrames is a private tool its owner uses to edit short videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy policy

HyperFrames uses Google sign-in only to act on the YouTube channel of the person who connected it: to upload that person's videos, set their titles and descriptions, read their upload status and statistics, and post or read comments on those videos.

  • No personal data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.
  • The Google access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent to any other service.
  • Data from Google APIs is used only for the purposes above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
